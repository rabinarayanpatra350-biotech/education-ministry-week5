# Education Ministry Internship — Stakeholder Engagement & Coordination

**Week 5 Task** | Education Ministry Internship, Coimbatore | Rabi Narayan Patra | 27 September 2026

A practitioner's report on the stakeholder ecosystem behind the foundational literacy and numeracy (FLN) project built across Weeks 3–4, mapped outward from the Class 3 child.

## The ecosystem (three tiers)

| Tier | Stakeholders | Verified figures |
|------|-------------|--------------------|
| **School** | Teachers; parents and the SMC; head teacher; Cluster Resource Person | 1.13 lakh teachers trained; SMCs are 75% parents and 50% women (RTE Sec. 21) |
| **District** | CEO/DEO/BEO; Block Resource Centres and DIET; Samagra Shiksha unit | Monthly TNSED-based reviews; term training calendar |
| **State & national** | SCERT; management partner (Madhi); MoE/NIPUN Bharat; NITI Aayog | Academic Resource Group grew 15 → 85 members; ₹152 cr + ₹110 cr |

## Consultation machinery at full scale

- **March 2022**: Tamil Nadu convened all parents statewide — ~**52 lakh parents across 37,000 schools** in a single day — relaunching SMCs as *Nam Palli Nam Perumai*
- **March 2023**: six-day, nine-zone Ennum Ezhuthum awareness drive; parents called to every school; **₹1,800–2,500 per school routed through the SMC**
- **Term-wise trainings, monthly SMC meetings and district reviews** on a fixed calendar

## The feedback loop (documented)

Teachers voiced concerns about handbook workload → the department measured time-on-task, **cut teacher activities by 38%**, trained administrators on competency mastery, and clarified workbook integration. Channels: a **~65,000-teacher Telegram group**, monthly reviews, and the Holistic Report Card's mandatory **parent comments** (Oct 2025).

## Coordination mechanisms

- The **SMC as statutory coordination point** (parents + teachers + local authority)
- The **embedded management-partner model** (teams across sub-departments; joint Academic Resource Group)
- **NITI Aayog's recommended State and District Task Forces** headed by the Chief Secretary and District Magistrate (May 2026 roadmap)

## Communication materials inventory

Holistic Report Card · levelled colour-coded workbooks · TNSED dashboards · folk-form IEC (plays, songs) reaching **5.25 crore people** nationally · one-page briefs
