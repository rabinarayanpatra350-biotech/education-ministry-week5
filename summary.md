# Executive Summary — Week 5: Stakeholder Engagement & Coordination

**Education Ministry Internship, Coimbatore** | Rabi Narayan Patra | 27 September 2026

## What the week did

Mapped the FLN stakeholder ecosystem in three tiers from the Class 3 child outward; documented the consultation machinery (SMC meetings, term trainings, district reviews, the 52-lakh-parent statewide meeting of 2022 and the nine-zone 2023 awareness drive); reconstructed the documented feedback loop (teacher concerns → 38% activity reduction); analysed coordination mechanisms (SMC, embedded partner, NITI Aayog task forces); and inventoried communication materials with the communication disciplines of government work.

## Key verified numbers

- **1.13 lakh** primary teachers trained; **~65,000** in the feedback Telegram group
- SMCs: **75% parents, 50% women** (RTE Sec. 21); head teacher as member-convener
- **52 lakh parents** across **37,000 schools** in the March 2022 statewide meeting
- Awareness drive (Mar 2023): 9 zones, **₹1,800–2,500 per school via the SMC**
- Feedback in action: teacher activities **cut 38%** after documented concerns
- Academic Resource Group: **15 → 85** members; IEC reach: **5.25 crore people** (FLN)
- NITI Aayog task forces: headed by **Chief Secretary (state)** and **District Magistrate (district)**
